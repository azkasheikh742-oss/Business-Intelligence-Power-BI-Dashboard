# Brazilian E-Commerce Business Intelligence Analysis By Azka Israr

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Business Intelligence project focused on analyzing Brazilian e-commerce business performance using the Olist dataset. This project transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and Power BI dashboards.

## Business Objectives
The primary objectives of this analysis are to:
- Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Understand order fulfilment and order status distribution.
- Investigate payment methods and transaction values.
- Reconcile product sales, freight charges, and recorded payments.
- Identify financial discrepancies requiring further investigation.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Measures, KPIs, and financial calculations.
- **Data Modeling:** Relationships between transactional and dimension tables.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and portfolio hosting.

## Dataset
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

The dataset contains Brazilian e-commerce transactions, including orders, customers, products, payments, and reviews.

## Key Findings

- Total product sales amounted to approximately R$13.59 million.
- Total freight charges amounted to approximately R$2.25 million.
- Expected order charges (product sales + freight) amounted to approximately R$15.84 million.
- Total recorded payment value was approximately R$16.01 million.
- The overall payment reconciliation variance was approximately R$165.32K.
- Cancelled and unavailable orders accounted for approximately 97.82% of the overall positive variance in the order-status breakdown.
- Credit card payments represented the largest payment method by transaction value.

**Important:** Payment reconciliation differences indicate discrepancies requiring further investigation. They do not independently establish missing funds, retained payments, or unprocessed refunds.

## Business Intelligence Approach

The project followed an end-to-end BI workflow:

1. Data extraction and inspection.
2. Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query.
3. Data modeling and relationship creation.
4. Development of DAX measures and KPIs.
5. Interactive dashboard development.
6. Financial reconciliation and discrepancy investigation.
7. Business insights and recommendations.
